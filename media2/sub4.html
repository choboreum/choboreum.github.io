DESGIN

ACNE STUDIOS'S DESGIN

Svenskt Tenn #01

Svenskt Tenn is a Swedish interior design store, founded in 1924 in Stockholm by Estrid Ericson, an art teacher and pewter artist from Hjo, Sweden.

Svenskt Tenn #02

Since 1928, the company has been appointed as a royal warrant.

Svenskt Tenn #03

Today, it is owned by the Kjell and Märta Beijer Foundation and all profit generated is donated to research in areas such as environmental sustainability, genetics, biomedicine and pharmaceuticals.

Svenskt Tenn #04

Inspired by the British Arts and Crafts designer William Morris, Ericson held the highest esteem for work that was crafted by hand.

Svenskt Tenn #05

Today, large portion of Josef Frank's furniture is made at the same carpentry shops in Småland and Södermanland that have been producing them since the 1950s.

Svenskt Tenn #06

The glass is made at, among other manufactories, the celebrated Reijmyre glassworks in Östergötland.

Svenskt Tenn #07

All of Svenskt Tenn's textiles are made from 100 percent cotton and linen.

Svenskt Tenn #08

The pewter workshops in Western Götaland in Sweden are also counted among Svenskt Tenn's long-time suppliers.

Svenskt Tenn #09

Today, eighty percent of Svenskt Tenn's range consists of products that are of its own design.

Svenskt Tenn #10

Frank's designs are highly esteemed not the least among today's young and established designers and his textiles are a source of inspiration for many contemporary print designers both in Sweden and other countries.

Svenskt Tenn #11

The Spirit of Estrid Ericson and Josef Frank is still preserved by Svenskt Tenn

Svenskt Tenn #12

Creative concept and direction for Svenskt Tenn, Autumn 2019

Svenskt Tenn #13

By the early 1930s, Svenskt Tenn has gained recognition as the most fashionable interior design store in Stockholm.

Svenskt Tenn #14

Estrid Ericson commissions the Swedish functionalist architects Uno Åhrén and Björn Trägårdh as Svenskt Tenn’s unique range of furniture and interior designs take form.

Svenskt Tenn #15

Estrid Ericson’s sense of beauty and quality evolve in tact with her wanderlust, and shopping expeditions take her to North and South America, England, France and Italy.

Svenskt Tenn #16

A careful renewal of the product range was coordinated with window displays and marketing. Collaborations with selected new designers as well as art and design schools are established.

Svenskt Tenn #17

Creative concept, direction for Svenskt Tenn, Winter 2019
